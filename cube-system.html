CUBE SYSTEM

One 300 mm Cube.
Every Room.

The cube is not just a form. It is a construction system for adaptation, customisation and retrofit: one module that becomes walls, storage and furniture, and comes apart as easily as it goes together.

01 — WHY CUBE

  1. 01

    ADAPTATION

    Rooms rebuild as needs change, without construction work.

  2. 02

    CUSTOMISATION

    Panels, functions and sizes mix per person, not per catalogue.

  3. 03

    RETROFIT

    Installs inside existing homes. No one has to move out.
