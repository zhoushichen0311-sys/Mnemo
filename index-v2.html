ABOUT

Homes That Change
With You.

Mnemosyne transforms existing homes into adaptive living environments that respond to
changing physical, sensory, and cognitive needs. Through flexible spatial planning and
modular design, the home evolves over time to support greater independence, comfort,
and confidence in everyday life.

01 — MOBILITY

Room to move

When wheels replace steps, corridors widen,
thresholds flatten and storage sinks to
reachable heights — without moving house.

02 — VISION

Edges that glow

For fading eyesight: luminous frames,
high-contrast surfaces and glare-free light.
A home you navigate by its own outlines.

03 — MEMORY

A layout that stays

Familiar arrangements are preserved and
quietly restored. The home keeps its shape
in mind — so its resident doesn’t have to.
